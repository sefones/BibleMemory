How to use Verse Card

Tap a question below to see the steps.

How do I add my first verse?
  1. On the first page, tap Find a verse.
  2. Type a verse like John 3:16, or tap a topic such as Prayer.
  3. Tap the verse you want.
  4. Wait a moment while the words appear. Then tap Save verse.

If the words don't appear, see "The words didn't fill in" below.

How do I practice a verse?
  1. On the first page, tap Practice a random verse (the app picks for you) or Practice a specific verse (you pick).
  2. Type the verse from memory.
  3. If you get a word wrong, the app shows what you had right and the next word. Keep going.
  4. Stuck? Slide Hint fullness to the right to see more of the verse.
  5. Tap Listen to hear the verse read out loud.
Can I use my own Bible (NIV, ESV, etc.)?
  1. Tap Add a new verse.
  2. Tap Look it up on Bible.com. A new page opens.
  3. Find your verse, then copy it (verse, reference, and link all together is fine).
  4. Come back to the app and paste it into the big box. The book, chapter, verse, and version fill in for you.
  5. Tap Save verse.
The words didn't fill in

The app can only fill in the words for these Bibles: KJV, WEB, ASV, and YLT (New Testament only). It also needs the internet.

  1. Change Version to KJV or WEB, then tap Fill in verse text.
  2. Or use the Bible.com steps above to paste the words yourself.
How do I keep my verses safe or move them to a new phone?

Your verses are saved only in this browser, on this device. If you clear your browser's data or switch phones, they are gone, so make a backup now and then.

  1. On the first page, find Your saved verses and tap Export. A backup file is saved.
  2. To restore, tap Import and choose that file.
  3. To send your verses to a friend, tap Share.
How do I add many verses at once?

Tap Add a new verse, then Bulk-import (.txt), and choose a plain text file. Write each verse like this, with a blank line between verses:

John 3:16 | KJV For God so loved the world... Psalm 23:1 | KJV The LORD is my shepherd...

The line with the version (| KJV) is optional. Files made by Export already look like this.

What do the little ? buttons do?

Tap any ? in the app for a short explanation of that button. Tap anywhere else to close it.

Tips
  • A few minutes a day works better than one long session.
  • On your phone, use your browser's share or menu button to add this page to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.
